Ethics

This project clones voices, and that capability is the subject of the presentation rather than a side effect of it. These are the parts the audience can act on.

Consent is enforced in code
A voice without a consent record is refused before anything loads — at startup, and again on a mid-talk swap. It has refused since the first week.
Every demo clip announces itself
“Questa voce è sintetica” at the start, the end, and unpredictable interior points chosen with secrets.SystemRandom and moved to the quietest nearby sample. A fixed pattern can be trimmed out; this cannot.
This pipeline does not watermark
Perth recovers Chatterbox's mark perfectly and finds nothing in Kokoro + Seed-VC. No watermark means only that whatever made this did not mark it — not that the audio is real. Detection threshold 0.75.
